### Key Features (Part 2)

**6. In-App Messaging**
This feature allows tenants and landlords to communicate directly within the platform to discuss room details, negotiate terms, or schedule viewings. It provides value to the user by keeping all rental-related conversations in one secure place, ensuring a seamless communication process without the need to rely on external messaging applications.

**7. Reviews and Ratings**
Tenants can leave a star rating (from 1 to 5) and written feedback for rooms they have rented or landlords they have interacted with. This feature is highly useful because it builds a transparent and trustworthy community, helping future tenants make informed decisions while encouraging landlords to maintain high-quality services.

**8. Notifications System**
Users receive timely system alerts for important activities, such as incoming messages or when their property listings have been approved by the admin. This ensures that both tenants and landlords stay informed and can respond promptly, significantly improving the overall interaction efficiency and user engagement.

**9. Moderation and Administration**
Administrators are provided with a dedicated dashboard to review, approve, or reject newly posted room listings, as well as manage user account statuses. This provides immense value by maintaining a safe platform environment, preventing spam, and protecting tenants from fraudulent or low-quality listings.

**10. Statistics and Dashboard**
This feature displays basic statistical metrics, such as the total number of active listings, user counts, and total page views, presented through simple visual charts. It is useful for administrators to monitor overall platform growth and for landlords to easily track the performance and reach of their rental postings.

### AI Feature

**AI-Powered Accommodation Assistant**  
To make finding a room easier, Roomify includes a smart AI chatbot powered by a Large Language Model (LLM). The main goal of this feature is to help users find their ideal accommodation just by chatting with the system, making the search process feel like talking to a real human assistant.

Instead of clicking through many complex search filters, users can simply type what they need in everyday language. For example, a user can type, "I want a room near the University of Science for under 3 million VND that has a parking space". The AI will understand this sentence, pick out the important details, and immediately show the best matching rooms. In addition, the chatbot acts as a helpful guide that can answer common questions about how to rent a room, contact the landlord, or handle deposit policies.

This AI feature provides real, practical value by saving users a lot of time and effort. Renters do not have to struggle with manual searches, and landlords get their rooms shown to the exact people who need them. Overall, the smart chatbot transforms the room-hunting experience into a highly personalized, fast, and friendly journey for everyone using the Roomify platform.
