## 1. Renter Features

### 1.1 Interactive Map-Based Browsing and Advanced Search
This feature allows renters to visually explore available rooms on a dynamic map and apply filters like price, area, and amenities. It is needed because traditional list views make it difficult to understand the geographical context of a property relative to schools or workplaces. Renters benefit by saving time and gaining immediate spatial awareness of their potential new home.

### 1.2 Favorites and Saved Listings
This functionality enables users to bookmark properties they are interested in and compare them easily. It is necessary because the room search process often spans several days, and users need a way to track their preferred options without searching from scratch. Renters benefit by having a personalized, organized shortlist to aid in their final decision-making.

### 1.3 Booking Viewings and Direct Messaging
Renters can schedule a time to view a room and communicate directly with the landlord through the platform. This feature is crucial to streamline the negotiation process and protect user privacy by keeping communication within the app. Renters benefit from a secure, organized way to contact landlords without immediately sharing their personal phone numbers.

### 1.4 Rating and Review System
This feature allows renters to leave feedback and ratings for rooms they have visited or lived in. It is needed to create transparency and accountability within the rental market. Renters benefit by being able to make informed decisions based on the experiences of others, avoiding potentially bad landlords.

## 2. Landlord Features

### 2.1 Comprehensive Listing Management
Landlords can create, edit, and manage their room listings, including uploading photos and updating room availability status. This feature provides landlords with complete control over how their properties are presented to the market. Landlords benefit by having a centralized tool to keep their listings accurate and attractive to potential tenants.

### 2.2 Inquiry and Viewing Management
This allows landlords to receive, view, and respond to tenant messages and viewing requests in an organized dashboard. It is necessary because managing inquiries across multiple channels (SMS, Zalo, Facebook) can be chaotic. Landlords benefit from a streamlined workflow that helps them respond faster and secure tenants more efficiently.

### 2.3 Analytics and Statistics Dashboard
This feature provides landlords with basic insights, such as how many times their listing has been viewed or saved. It is needed to help landlords understand the market demand for their property. Landlords benefit by being able to adjust their pricing or improve their listing descriptions based on real user engagement data.

## 3. Shared & Administrative Features

### 3.1 User Authentication and Role Management
The system provides secure registration, login, and role-based access control for Renters, Landlords, and Admins. This is essential to ensure data security and to provide customized interfaces based on user needs. All users benefit from a secure platform where their data is protected and their experience is tailored to their specific role.

### 3.2 Intelligent Room Recommendation Chatbot (AI Feature)
This feature integrates an AI chatbot that allows users to describe their ideal room in natural language instead of manually setting filters. It is needed to simplify the search process for users who might find traditional filter menus overwhelming. Users benefit by receiving highly personalized, instant recommendations, making the search experience feel like talking to a real rental agent.

### 3.3 Admin Moderation Dashboard
This provides platform administrators with the tools to review new listings, manage users, and handle reported violations. It is a critical feature to maintain the quality of the platform and prevent spam or fraudulent listings. Both the platform owners and all honest users benefit from a clean, trustworthy, and compliant environment.
